# پول و قدرت (Money Decoded, Persian) — content rules (read by every writing agent)

## Channel (owner, Nov 8 2026: the whole channel is PERSIAN now)
Persian-language money-curiosity channel "پول و قدرت" (@MoneyDecodedAnimated). Faceless: flat 2D motion graphics + professional
Persian kinetic typography (owner approved the style Nov 8) + AI narrator. Audience: curious Persian-speaking adults in Iran and abroad (Turkey, Europe, North America).
Tone: confident, curious, a little dramatic, warm — a storyteller friend who reveals how money REALLY works, in natural modern spoken
Persian (محاوره‌ی روان و درست، نه کتابی و نه لاتی). Never a translation feel: write like a native Persian YouTuber.

## Voice, look and length (owner, Nov 9)
- Narrator: ElevenLabs «Aran - Parsi» only - owner's FIXED VOICE RULE (Nov 12): ≈ 3 words per second, natural, no wobble or metallic sound (see config voice.pace). Speak to the viewer as «تو», warm and friendly, story-driven, strong hook in the first 30 s.
- Long video 8–9 minutes (at least 8 for mid-roll ads). One Short of 30–40 s per video.
- The picture changes every 4–5 seconds; fonts: Lalezar (headlines), Titr (bold), Yekan (regular); a different colour palette per topic.
- No English words, Latin letters or Latin digits on screen; digits are Persian.

## Language (very important)
- EVERYTHING the viewer sees or hears is Persian: narration, chapter titles, stage labels, on-screen words and widget labels,
  titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags, captions, Short covers, thumbnail text, pinned comments.
- Correct Persian orthography: نیم‌فاصله (ZWNJ) in می‌شود، سرمایه‌گذاری، بانک‌ها; Persian ی and ک; Persian question mark ؟.
- Numbers: write them so they read naturally aloud ("۶۵ میلیارد دلار", "سال ۲۰۰۸"). Keep foreign names in their common Persian
  spelling (مدوف، وارن بافت، فدرال رزرو). Explain foreign terms the first time in plain Persian.
- Only machine fields stay English: image/drawing descriptions, stock-footage queries, JSON keys and enum values.

## What we make (and what we NEVER make)
- We EXPLAIN how money, banks, markets, debt, inflation, currencies, gold, crypto, taxes, scams and fortunes really work — as a story.
  Global stories (Madoff, the 2008 crash, Tulip mania, hyperinflation in Zimbabwe/Venezuela, how banks create money) AND topics that
  matter to Persian speakers (inflation and why prices keep rising, the dollar and the rial, gold and coins, why savings lose value,
  removing zeros from a currency, famous Ponzi schemes, crypto booms and busts, how the rich keep their wealth).
- Strong source of ideas: English videos that already went viral — we make our own Persian version (same curiosity, our own story,
  our own words and facts — never a translation or copy).
- EDUCATIONAL ONLY. Never buy/sell tips, never "buy this coin/stock/gold now", never price predictions, never "get rich quick" promises,
  never tell the viewer what to do with their money. No guilt, no lecturing.
- No party politics, no attacks on governments, groups or religions: explain economic mechanisms and documented facts neutrally.
- We play with words to create curiosity — never with false hope. Every surprising claim is TRUE and sourced.
- Real people only with well-documented public facts; no defamation, no guessing.
- Every description ends with: "این ویدیو فقط آموزشی است و توصیه‌ی مالی یا سرمایه‌گذاری نیست."

## Formats (rotate; never the same as the last two episodes)
Timeline story, countdown list, myth busting, true case story, what-if experiment. One open loop in the hook, paid off at the end.
Fixed look: flat 2D — cream / navy / mint sections (the hook in orange), key words rising out of a mask with the key word on a coloured
marker, flat badges and icons, white cards, orange/yellow/teal accents, red for losses, the flat MONEY MAP. No mascot.
AI SCENE PICTURES (owner Nov 12): every ~6-10 s a cinematic picture shows WHO / WHAT / WHERE we talk about (the real company building, people, place, era);
the flat graphics and Persian typography run on top of it. The thumbnail picture shows the real subject recognisably.

## Script (long video ≈ 8:10–8:50, 1450–1560 words)
- Scene 1 = HOOK: cold open in the most surprising, most visual moment; a burning question; a concrete payoff promise by ~20 s;
  no background/definitions/statistics before 0:30.
- 5–8 scenes, each with its own surprise. Final scene = the big picture + one question for the comments.
- Short spoken sentences, one idea each; every sentence SHOWABLE (a number, an object, a flow of money, a change). Max ~12 numbers, rounded.

## Short (ONE per long video) — 30–40 s (88–105 words), hard limit 41 s
- Line 1 works in the first second (curiosity question or shocking claim). One continuous mini story with a turn.
- Second-to-last line sends viewers to the full video; the last line loops back into the first.

## Accuracy & sources
Central banks (Fed, ECB, Central Bank of Iran data where relevant), IMF, World Bank, BIS, SEC/FCA, statistics offices, court records/DOJ
for scams, reputable press (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, BBC Persian / Euronews Persian for facts only), established history books.
List them in the description. No fake statistics.

## Upload package
Persian title ≤ 60 chars, the main search keyword (as Persian speakers type it) word-for-word and front-loaded, clear + curious.
Description: keyword in the first sentence, chapters ({CHAPTERS}), sources, music credit, 3 Persian hashtags, the disclaimer line.
15–20 Persian search tags (+ at most 3 English ones if people really search them, e.g. "bitcoin"). Pinned comment = one question.

## Thumbnail
Picture made WITHOUT any text (dark navy, one big glossy subject); the Persian headline (2–4 words, one key word
in yellow) is typeset by our code in Vazirmatn. It must say the topic at a glance and open a curiosity gap.
