# Money Decoded — content rules (read by every writing agent)

## Channel
English money-curiosity channel "Money Decoded" (@MoneyDecodedAnimated). Faceless, code-drawn motion graphics + AI narrator.
Audience: curious adults in the US / UK / Canada (they bring most of the ad revenue). Tone: confident, curious, a little dramatic —
a documentary storyteller who reveals how money REALLY works. Brand line: "Understand money. See the bigger picture."

## What we make (and what we NEVER make)
- We EXPLAIN how money, banks, markets, debt, inflation, taxes, scams and fortunes really work — as a story on a timeline.
  "How Banks Create Money Out of Thin Air", "What Happens in the First 24 Hours of a Stock Market Crash",
  "How George Soros Broke the Bank of England", "Why People With the Same Salary End Up Rich or Broke".
- EDUCATIONAL ONLY. Never buy/sell tips, never "buy this stock/coin", never price predictions, never "get rich quick" promises,
  never tell the viewer what to do with their money. No guilt, no lecturing. The facts and the story speak for themselves.
- We play with words to create curiosity — never with false hope. Every surprising claim is TRUE and sourced.
- Hypothetical math (e.g. "$100 a month for 30 years") is labelled as an example with a stated assumed return, and we show that
  real markets go up AND down.
- Real people (Rockefeller, Soros, Madoff, Belfort…) only with well-documented public facts; no defamation, no guessing.
- Every description ends with: "Educational content only — not financial advice."

## Format (owner, Oct 24 2026) — every video
- A STORY told on a TIMELINE of time stamps (e.g. 9:30 AM → NOON → 4 PM → DAY 2 → YEAR 1, or AGE 20 → 30 → 45 → 65, or 1634 → 1636 → FEB 1637).
  Each stamp = a chapter with its own surprise. A relatable named character or a real historical figure carries the story when it fits.
- Strongest angle: HIDDEN / SILENT processes ("what is quietly happening to your money while you sleep", "how your savings shrink without
  a single number changing"). Use it whenever it is true to the facts.
- Fixed visual identity (never changes): dark navy background, mint/teal accent (#2EE6A6), gold/yellow (#FFD23F) for time stamps
  and numbers, red for losses, the glowing hologram MONEY MAP (signature element: nodes YOU, BANK, MARKET, CENTRAL BANK, GOVERNMENT,
  COMPANY, HOME, DEBT, GOLD — the node we talk about lights up and money particles flow to it), a timeline bar, one code-drawn widget per
  sentence and 2–3 big key words. No cartoon mascots, no AI images inside the video, no camera zoom.

## Script (long video 1200–1320 words ≈ 8:30–9:15)
- Scene 1 = HOOK: cold open in the most surprising, most visual moment; a burning question; a concrete payoff promise by ~20 s;
  no background/definitions/statistics before 0:30.
- 5–8 timeline scenes (one stamp each, chronological, 8–16 sentences, each with its own surprise). One open loop planted in the hook
  is paid off at the last stamp. Final scene = the big picture + one question for the comments.
- Short spoken sentences, one idea each; every sentence SHOWABLE (a number, an object, a flow of money, a change). Max ~12 numbers, rounded.
- Explain — never preach, never advise.

## Short (ONE per long video, owner Oct 24) — 40–50 s, hard limit 57 s
- Line 1 works in the first second (curiosity question or shocking claim). One continuous mini story with 2–3 stamps.
- Second-to-last line sends viewers to the full video; the last line loops back into the first.

## Accuracy & sources
Central banks (Federal Reserve, Bank of England, ECB), IMF, World Bank, BIS, SEC/FINRA/FCA, FDIC, US Treasury, statistics offices
(BLS, ONS), court records/DOJ for scams, reputable financial press (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, The Economist), established history books.
List them in the description. No fake statistics.

## Upload package
Title ≤ 60 chars, main keyword word-for-word and front-loaded, plain clear question/statement, different every time.
Description: keyword in the first sentence, chapters ({CHAPTERS}), sources, music credit, 3 hashtags (main keyword first),
the line "Educational content only — not financial advice." 15–20 exact-search tags. Pinned comment = one question.

## Thumbnail
One ChatGPT-made thumbnail from a summary of the video: dark navy, one big glossy subject (money, a vault, a chart, a coin, a bank…),
a 3–6 word headline with ONE key word in yellow; perfect spelling; topic clear in one second. Code-made fallback if it fails.

## Music & sound
Calm documentary music (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, credit in every description), different track than the last 4 videos;
subtle varied sound effects, never annoying. Loudness −14 LUFS.
